What We Build From Thar Desert Silica Sand

Solar Glass Grade Silica

99%+ SiO2 purity extracted and beneficiated from Jaisalmer silica sand deposits for photovoltaic glass manufacturing.

Industrial Ceramics

High-temperature refractory materials and silicates refined from uncharacterized desert sand for heavy industrial and ceramic applications.

Construction Composites

Eco-friendly supplementary cementitious materials offering sustainable alternatives to river sand and Portland cement.

Sand Battery Thermal Storage

Long-duration thermal energy storage systems utilizing high thermal capacity Thar sand up to 600°C for industrial heat and grid power.

Industrial Sand Processing Pipeline

  1. Raw Jaisalmer Sand Sourcing from licensed miners
  2. High-intensity Magnetic Separation for iron oxide removal
  3. AI and ML Spectroscopic Quality Grading using XRF data
  4. Industrial Grade 99%+ SiO2 Output Packaging
  5. B2B Logistics Supply Chain to glass and ceramic manufacturers

Why TharUrja

The Thar Desert spans over 200,000 km² of silica-rich sand. With a global silica sand market exceeding ₹17B+ and a 40% CAGR in online industrial silica growth, TharUrja bridges the gap between raw desert sand potential and India's accelerating demand for solar-grade silica.
